## Abilities

### Divination Savant [^school-of-divination]

1/2 gold/time to transcribe divination spells to spellbook

### Portent / Greater Portent [^school-of-divination]

Roll three d20s after long rest. Once per turn replace any attack roll, saving throw, or ability check made by you or a creature that you can see. *before the roll*

### Expert Divination [^school-of-divination]

Divination spells restore a slot one level lower (max 5th level slot recovered)

### The Third Eye [^school-of-divination]

Once per short rest (or until incapacitated) choose:

  * Darkvision. You gain darkvision out to a range of 60 feet.
  * Ethereal Sight. You can see into the Ethereal Plane within 60 feet of you.
  * Greater Comprehension. You can read any language.
  * See Invisibility. You can see invisible creatures and objects within 10 feet of you that are within line of sight.


### Arcane recovery [^wizard]

Once per day after short rest recover half wizard level (rounded up) (+1 [^arcane_grimoire]) of level 5 or lower slot.

### Cantrip formulas [^wizard]

Swap out a wizard cantrip after long rest

### Telekinetic [^feat-telekinetic]

  * +30ft Mage hand. Invisible. No verbal/somatic
  * As bonus action can shove one visable creature within 30ft by 5ft (toward/away) (str save vs 8+prof+int bonus)

### Telepathic [^feat-telepathic]

  * Speak telepathically to creatures within 60ft. Can use known languages only.
  * Detect thoughts known. Can cast free once per long rest.
